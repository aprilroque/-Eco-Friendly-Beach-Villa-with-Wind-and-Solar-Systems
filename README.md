# Description 

The increasing demand for renewable energy has encouraged the use of sustainable technologies in modern infrastructure and tourism industries. Coastal regions have strong potential for renewable energy generation due to abundant sunlight and wind resources.

This project focuses on integrating solar panels and wind turbines into the design of a private beach villa. The system is designed to minimize dependence on fossil fuels while maintaining energy efficiency, environmental sustainability, and modern living standards.

The study demonstrates how renewable energy technologies can support eco-tourism and sustainable coastal development.
